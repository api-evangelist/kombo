Kombo launched Create Employee with Staffing Entities, a feature that matches new hires to correct legal entities, cost centers, and positions across enterprise HRIS systems. It unifies positions, requisitions, and jobs into one data model and prefills about 80% of employee creation forms across 40+ HRIS providers, with Workday and UKG Pro live now.
